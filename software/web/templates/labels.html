Music Box

Card labels

← home

Put album covers on a sheet of 60 × 60 mm sticker labels, one per card. Pick a favourite for each label and its cover is fetched from Sonos; add your own picture instead if a cover looks fuzzy.

  1. Pick a favourite (or choose a picture) for each label you want to print.
  2. Press Make the sheet — it opens as a PDF.
  3. Print it at 100% / actual size, no "fit to page".
